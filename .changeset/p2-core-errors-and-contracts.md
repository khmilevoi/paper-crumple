Errors, the abort sentinel and the contract surface. `CrumpleError` and the eleven error classes;
the `ABORTED` registry sentinel with `isAborted` over its four normative shapes; `taggedError`,
`attempt`, `findCause`, `partition`, `matchError`, `unwrap`, `unwrapAsync` and `assertSingleCore`;
the nine named result aliases of §10.2; and the type-only contract surface — `GlContext`,
`SheetRenderer`, `MotionSource`, the three view targets, `StageOptions`, `PoseRef` and the
`Events` map — split between the stable root and `@paper-crumple/core/unstable`.
